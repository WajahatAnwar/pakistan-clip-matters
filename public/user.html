Type any word or phrase to find all videos mentioning it

👤

🎙️

Voice Search

💡 Search Examples

Example 1: Word Search
"economy"
→ Finds all moments where anyone says "economy" in any video
Example 2: Meaning Search
"discussion about financial policies"
→ Finds relevant segments even if exact words aren't used (like "monetary decisions", "budget planning")
Example 3: Speaker Search
Speaker: "Hafiz Naeem"
→ Shows all video segments where Hafiz Naeem is speaking
Example 4: Combined Search
"education" + Speaker: "Chairman"
→ Finds where the Chairman talks about education topics

✨ Smart Typo Handling

Don't worry about spelling! Search for "Muhamad" and it will find "Muhammad", "Mohammed", etc.

📋 Understanding Your Results

When you search, you'll see results organized by video:

  • 📺
    Video Title & Thumbnail
    Easily identify which video contains your result
  • ⏱️
    Timestamp
    Exact time when the content appears (click to jump directly)
  • 👤
    Speaker Name
    Who is speaking at that moment
  • 📝
    Transcript Preview
    Text of what was said, with search terms highlighted
  • ⭐
    Relevance Score
    How closely the result matches your search

💪 Pro Tips

  • 1️⃣
    Use meaning search for broad topics - Search "political discussion" instead of specific words
  • 2️⃣
    Use word search for specific quotes - When you know exactly what was said
  • 3️⃣
    Combine speaker + topic - Find what a specific person said about a topic
  • 4️⃣
    Check your search history - Quickly repeat previous searches
  • 5️⃣
    Use voice search - Faster than typing, especially for Urdu queries
