📋 What is Clip Matters?

Clip Matters is an intelligent content management system. It transforms how organizations handle digital content from storage and organization to search and sharing.

💡 In Simple Terms

Think of it as a "Google for your videos" - but smarter! You can search inside videos to find exactly who said what, when they said it, and create clips instantly. No more watching hours of footage to find one quote!

⚡ What You Can Do as Admin

  • ✓
    Upload & Process Videos
    Connect to Dropbox, select videos, and let AI process them automatically. Videos are uploaded to YouTube (unlisted) for easy playback.
  • ✓
    Train Speaker Recognition
    Upload voice samples of key people (leaders, speakers, hosts). The system will automatically identify them in all videos!
  • ✓
    View Dashboard Analytics
    See processing stats, most searched terms, user activity, and system health at a glance.
  • ✓
    Manage Users
    Create accounts for your team, set permissions, and control who can access the platform.
  • ✓
    Bulk Video Processing
    Process multiple videos at once - perfect for handling large video archives.
  • ✓
    Monitor Processing Status
    Track which videos are pending, processing, or completed with detailed progress indicators.
